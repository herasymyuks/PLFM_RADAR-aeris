## 4. Silkscreen

Fix applied in BETA: `tools/beta_silk_nudge.py` moved 43 reference texts to the nearest free position (spiral search, 0.05 mm step, ≤ 3.0 mm from the part, avoiding pads + mask expansion + 0.1 mm, other texts and silk outlines). Log: `silk_nudges.json`. Remaining conflicts (103 texts, `silk_conflicts_remaining.json`) have no free position within 3 mm on this 0201-dense board. Recommendation for the production release: hide the reference designators of 0201/0402 passives on silk (assembly uses `exports/drawings/*_assembly_top.pdf` with the F.Fab layer) — not done in BETA because it changes the drawing content the designer reviews.

Footprint silk outlines over pads (EAGLE tPlace polygons) are footprint-library content: not moved (would require editing every footprint); the fabricator's CAM clips silkscreen on exposed copper.

## 5. Solder-mask bridges

The EAGLE packages have 0 mm mask expansion; KiCad's `solder_mask_bridge` test flags apertures whose webs would be thinner than the "min mask web" (project default 0.0 mm → every adjacent-pad pair of different nets). PCBWay's ENIG process guarantees 0.1 mm dams; where the gap is smaller the CAM will remove the dam (gang relief) — standard for 0201 and 0.4/0.5 mm pitch QFN/LGA. No change made; the designer may define per-footprint mask expansion in the production revision.
