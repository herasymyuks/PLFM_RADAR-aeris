## How to close them (manual, KiCad PCB editor)

1. Open `POWER_SUPPLY.kicad_pcb`, Inspect → Design Rules Checker → *Run DRC*, use the *Unconnected Items* tab to jump to each entry.
2. Zone ↔ zone gaps of `VIN`/`GND`/`+xVy`: route a 2.0 mm (VIN) / 1.0 mm (others) track between the two pours on F.Cu, or extend one pour outline so that it reaches the other (Edit zone → drag outline), then *Refill zones*.
3. GND pad ↔ GND gaps: add a 0.6/0.3 mm via next to the pad into the B.Cu plane where the DRC clearance allows it; where the plane island below is isolated, move the autorouted B.Cu track that cuts it (all autorouted tracks are listed in `ses_merge.json` / `ses_merge_pass2.json` and can be identified by their 0.25 mm width).
4. Re-run DRC until *Unconnected items* = 0, then `bash beta/pcb/tools/beta_export_package.sh POWER_SUPPLY`.
